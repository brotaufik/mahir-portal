The most useful precedent

A delivery rider can already contest an algorithm

Since 31 March 2026 the Gig Workers Act gives workers the right not to be deactivated by an algorithm without recourse, and a tribunal to hear the complaint. KESUMA states it gives greater protection to 3.45 million gig workers.
Parliament accepted the principle. We ask for it to be extended to every Malaysian facing a high-stakes automated decision: in health, in credit, in welfare, in school placement.
Why does a loan applicant deserve less protection than a food delivery rider?

The framework

MAHIR: five rules, five words

Mahir means skilled in Malay. The plan already has a pillar called Trust via Responsible Governance, listing enablers E11 to E14. Good ideas, but nobody remembers a code.

I — Everything stays on the record

How it works

Five rules are one governance cycle

We manage the risk, we hold someone accountable, we keep a human in command, we protect the system’s integrity, and we always come back to dignity.
We are not asking the country to start over. Enablers E11 to E14 are already written. We are asking for one thing: adopt MAHIR as the plain-language version of that pillar, so every Malaysian can understand it and hold it to account.

M · the human gate

AI recommends. Humans decide.

The assurance body already exists. The Malaysia AI Safety Institute, MY-AISafe, was launched inside AI Malaysia Berhad on 28 July 2026 with a mandate for safety evaluation and red-teaming.
MAHIR gives it a gate to stand at: we propose its safety certificate becomes a condition of deployment for high-risk public sector AI, not a recommendation agencies can ignore.
