R · sequence

Reskill before retrenchment, not after

The usual sequence is the reverse: AI is introduced, employees are displaced, retraining is offered afterwards if at all. By then the income is gone and the household has already absorbed the shock.
Fund it by tying the reskilling budget to a published displacement estimate per sector, routed through the HRD Corp levy employers already pay. Automate the task. Keep the person.
