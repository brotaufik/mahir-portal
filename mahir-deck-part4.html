I · traceability

Everything stays on the record

Keep the record: the system, the data used, the recommendation made, the human approval, and every change since. Then monitor continuously for incidents, bias and model drift.
  • The system that made the recommendation
  • The data it was trained and run on
  • The recommendation itself
  • The human approval that followed
  • Every change made since deployment

R · rights

Respect the person

Anyone affected by an AI decision keeps three things. Malaysia has already accepted this principle in law through the Gig Workers Act 2025.
  • Privacy
  • Human agency
  • The ability to challenge a consequential decision
R · inclusion

Include every Malaysian

The benefit cannot stop at the Klang Valley, large corporations and the digitally confident. This follows the plan’s own direction: AI Nation 2030 is explicit that the benefits must be inclusive, not adoption for its own sake.
  • Rural communities
  • Elderly citizens
  • Persons with disabilities
  • Low-income households
  • SMEs and people with limited digital literacy
R · sequence

Reskill before retrenchment, not after

The usual sequence
  • AI is introduced
  • Employees are displaced
  • Retraining is offered afterwards, if at all
  • By then the income is gone and the household has absorbed the shock
The MAHIR sequence
  • Announce the implementation
  • Identify the vulnerable tasks
  • Run a skills-gap assessment
  • Reskill pre-emptively and redesign the job
  • Deploy human and AI together
Fund it by tying the reskilling budget to a published displacement estimate per sector, routed through the HRD Corp levy employers already pay. Automate the task. Keep the person.

See it in one case

The same hiring system, run two ways

Without MAHIR
  • AI screens and rejects applicants
  • Hidden bias goes unnoticed
  • Applicants are never told why
  • The company blames the software
  • Result: nobody is answerable
With MAHIR
  • M: classified high risk, assessed and validated before deployment
  • A: a named officer owns the system
  • H: no rejection by opaque automation alone, a human review can be requested
  • I: every rejection traceable, with a complete audit trail
  • R: affected staff reskilled before the system goes live
  • Result: a named human answers for the outcome
