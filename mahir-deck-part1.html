The governance half

The half that is still being written

Malaysia already has its governance principles. They live in the National Guidelines on AI Governance and Ethics, issued by MOSTI in September 2024. Seven strong principles, adopted voluntarily.
  • Fairness
  • Reliability, safety and control
  • Privacy and security
  • Inclusiveness
  • Transparency
  • Accountability
  • Pursuit of human benefit and happiness
The government is now converting them into law through the AI Governance Bill, in public consultation with completion targeted end 2026. MAHIR is our proposal for what that law should require and measure.
