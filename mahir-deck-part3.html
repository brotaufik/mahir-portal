Somebody’s name is on it

Every high-risk AI system has a clearly identified responsible organisation and an accountable officer. Never “the algorithm decided.”

WhoWhenWhat they answer for

Deploying company or ministryWhile it is in useOwns how the system is actually used

Machines can make mistakes. Under MAHIR, people are still the ones who answer for them.

H · the checkpoint

The final call belongs to a person

Any decision affecting a citizen’s rights, safety or welfare passes through a human checkpoint before it is finalised. Someone with the authority to override, to suspend, to reject.
Not a machine that decides your fate in silence, but a person who can still stop and think it through. H sits at the centre of MAHIR, and that is not an accident.
